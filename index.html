02 The Equity You've Built

Three Strengths Already In Place

All three are earned, all three are working. Any new video would build on what's already here.

Operator equity. Think Consulting has been in continuous operation since 2004, and today employs over 250 consultants and professionals across six service practices. The firm publishes a 90% project success rate, a 78% client renewal rate, and was named one of America's Fastest-Growing Private Companies in August 2025. Twelve named leaders on the Leadership page run those practices, with Tony Gruebl as the founder and CEO and Joe Poling as President and CRO. The track record is unambiguous, and every one of those numbers is a story the firm has earned the right to tell.

Voice equity. The Thinking Out Loud blog publishes regularly on real subjects: marketing, enrollment strategy, NAB Show trends, the firm's own growth recognition. Each piece carries a clear point of view rather than service-page copy. The LinkedIn company page has 53,181 followers and posts often enough that the audience is already in the habit of hearing from the firm. The voice is established. What's missing is the format.

Cultural equity. The six industries on the Sectors page (Media, Government, Venture, Healthcare, Cyber, Education) are the kinds of clients whose work shapes how their sectors operate. The firm's recent feed shows it investing in its own people too, with internal HR investment posts, hire announcements that pull real engagement, National Volunteer Week activity, and a presence at the NAB Show. The fabric is there to film.

The one thing missing from the public presence is long-form video that comes back week after week: leader-led teaching from the bench, client hero films from inside the engagements, documentary treatment of the firm's case work. That is a format gap, not a quality gap, and it is what the Legendeering framework on the next page is built to close.

03 Lead With Value

The Legendeering Framework, In One Page

The methodology behind every Talex production. Five directives, one core principle.

Legendeering is Talex's proprietary strategy. The core principle is straightforward: lead with value, always. The brand earns trust by giving without expectation, and that trust grows over time because people keep choosing to watch.

Five directives drive it:

  • ·Give Without Expectation. Value delivered without strings. The kind of generosity that builds loyalty money cannot buy.
  • ·Be Relentlessly Authentic. Say true things. Show real work. Speak like a human being.
  • ·It's Not About You. It's About Them. The audience has to come first. It's all about their wants and needs and making something they will choose to watch.
  • ·Start With Story, Follow With Facts. Story gives information shape, context, and emotional weight. Facts alone rarely move people.
  • ·Consistency Is Key. Trust is built in moments, repeated over time.

Think Consulting are experts at solving hard operating problems for clients across six industries. Talex would add the long-form video: documentary films built on the work the firm is already doing. The three ideas below are what that looks like for a firm at Think's scale.

04 Three Concepts For Think Consulting

Three Long-Form Concepts For The Think Brand

Each one builds on equity Think has already earned. Each one is something only Think could make.

01
"Inside The Engagement"
A returning documentary series that follows one Think client through one real engagement as it unfolds. The client's CEO or operating leader tells the story in their own words from inside the work, while the Think Managing Director on the engagement appears as the strategic guide who helps the audience understand what the choices actually look like from the inside. Each season picks an industry from Think's six sectors (a Government modernization, a Healthcare integration, an Education enrollment turnaround) and the film is something the client can use indefinitely on their own channels while it doubles as the most credible portfolio piece Think could put in front of the next prospect in that sector.
02
"The Think Bench"
A teaching-format short series where each Managing Director takes one concept they've spent a career on and puts it in plain terms a client could act on. Tony on what twenty-two years of building a consulting firm has actually taught him about operating discipline. Joe on running a national practice. Alissa on what management consulting looks like when the engagement actually lands. You on the hiring and retention work that brought you to Think. It's the same expertise that fills the Thinking Out Loud blog, in a format the audience is more likely to watch all the way through.
03
"The Talent Question"
A returning documentary series anchored in the Human Capital Management practice you now lead. Each film follows one Think client through a specific talent decision (a leadership transition, a restructuring, a scaling moment, an executive search) with the client's CEO or COO on camera telling the story from inside the work. You appear as the Think guide who helps the audience understand what the choices actually look like before they are made. The audience this finds is every operating leader who has had to make the same decision and wondered who they should have called. The films double as the most credible portfolio piece the HCM practice could put in front of the next prospect, and as a film the client owns and can use indefinitely on their own channels.
